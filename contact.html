Frequently Asked Questions

Is EduConnect completely free for students?

Yes. EduConnect is developed as an open student Community Service Project (CSP) aimed at academic collaboration. There are zero fees or subscription paywalls.

How long does it take for an uploaded document to appear?

Submitted materials are reviewed by student moderators and administrator coordinators to confirm syllabus relevance and legibility before being approved into the public library.
