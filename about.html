Community Service Project (CSP)

Democratizing Academic Resources for Every Student

EduConnect is a student-driven initiative designed to address the fragmentation of learning materials across college student communities.

The Problem We Are Solving

Throughout our college terms, students face recurring challenges preparing for semester examinations, laboratory vivas, and competitive project presentations. High-quality subject lecture notes, previous question papers with model answers, and lab observation manuals are often scattered across disparate chat groups, expired temporary links, and private student archives.

Students entering a new semester or facing backlog revisions frequently struggle to identify trustworthy, syllabus-compliant study references. EduConnect bridges this information gap by providing a persistent, categorized web portal where knowledge is preserved and easily accessible to all learners.

Core Objectives

Centralized Academic Library

Catalog study documents by department, semester, subject, and resource category.

Faceted Instant Discovery

Quickly isolate required syllabus notes with multi-attribute filtering and live text search.

Peer-Powered Uploads

Empower students to share verified notes with moderation checks to ensure academic quality.

Universal Device Access

Fully responsive on mobile devices for campus reading and desktop PCs for extensive study sessions.

Technical Architecture & Stack

Frontend Layer
Vanilla HTML5, CSS3 Custom Properties, Responsive Flex/Grid, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), Fetch API.
Backend Layer
Java Spring Boot 3.x REST Controllers, Spring Security, JWT authentication, Multipart file streaming.
Database & Storage
MySQL Relational Database accessed strictly through Spring Data JPA repositories.

Commitment to Academic Integrity

EduConnect is designed exclusively to facilitate peer review, study preparation, and collaborative learning. We strictly forbid the dissemination of confidential examination leaks, commercial copyrighted materials, or unauthorized academic records. All content is subject to review by student moderators and administrator coordinators.
